The app converts the leaf image to an Enhanced Color Visibility index and relates that index to nitrogen. The calibration is described as fairly strong. The paper does not print the coefficient in the extract used here, and it notes that a fixed camera height of about 20 cm made the reading more stable. The app targeted Android 2.3 through 4.0.
