On 20 cassava leaves, with a SPAD-502 as the reference, the paper reports a coefficient of determination of 0.9681 and an RMSE of 0.014. The output is an NDVI-style combination of the red and near-infrared channels, sent over Bluetooth to an app named NDVI Reader.
