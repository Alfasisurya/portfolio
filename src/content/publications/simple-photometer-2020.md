The instrument uses a 633 nm LED, a TSL250 photodiode, a 3D-printed cuvette holder, and an Arduino Uno for acquisition. Against a UV-Vis 1800 spectrophotometer the paper reports a coefficient of determination of 0.98, with LOD and LOQ absorbance of 0.0267 and 0.0344.
