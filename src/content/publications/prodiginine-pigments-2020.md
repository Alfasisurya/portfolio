Strains PS1 and SB14 were isolated from two sites on Alor Island. The paper reports cycloprodigiosin, prodigiosin, and 2-methyl-3-hexyl-prodiginine as major pigments, plus three minor prodiginine derivatives that differ in alkyl-chain length. In a disk-diffusion test at 20 μg/mL, cycloprodigiosin showed the greatest inhibition of *Staphylococcus aureus*, 25.1 ± 0.55 mm.
