English rendering of the title: interpolation of nitrogen dioxide in Yogyakarta by ordinary kriging and inverse distance weighting. The city measured NO2 at some sites only. On the comparison in the paper, ordinary kriging had an RMSE of 0.4847 and inverse distance weighting had an RMSE of 0.5224.
