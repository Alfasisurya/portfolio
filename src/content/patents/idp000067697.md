The CV describes a wireless system that acquires digital images and predicts drought-tolerant soybean varieties with fuzzy logic and artificial neural networks. The assignee on the CV is Universitas Ma Chung. The grant year on the CV is 2020.

The patent PDFs stored with the working files are other people’s NIR applications. They do not name Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa, and they are not listed as his patents. The certificate for IDP000067697 itself was not in those files, so this page does not quote an official abstract or add a register link.
