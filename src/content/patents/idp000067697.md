Original title: *Metode dan Sistem Akuisisi Data Citra Digital Nirkabel untuk Prediksi Varietas Tanaman Kedelai Toleran Kekeringan Menggunakan Algoritma Fuzzy dan Jaringan Saraf Tiruan.*

The system captures two kinds of digital images over a wireless link. A human-operated control unit manages the camera interface, data storage and deletion, and the prediction routine. Leaf greenness is extracted from the images and converted quickly into an NDVI index. Drought tolerance is then predicted with either fuzzy logic or an artificial neural network, and the algorithm that gives the more accurate prediction from the two image types is selected. The method replaces the conventional approach of judging leaf greenness by eye.

The research behind it was published in 2015 as a paper on a simple multispectral sensor and artificial intelligence for screening drought-tolerant soybean.
