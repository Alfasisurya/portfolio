The system captures digital images of soybean plants over a wireless link and uses fuzzy logic and artificial neural networks to predict which varieties tolerate drought. The patent is held by Universitas Ma Chung and was granted in 2020.

The method grew out of the research published in 2015 on a simple multispectral sensor and artificial intelligence for screening drought-tolerant soybean.
