Between 2013 and 2021, the Ma Chung research group developed a series of methods that extract agronomic information from ordinary leaf images.

- **Mata Daun (2013)** estimates soybean-leaf nitrogen from an RGB photograph using an Enhanced Color Visibility index.
- **Fuzzy leaf color (2014)** is a phone application that converts soybean leaf color into a fertilizer-related color level.
- **Citrus anomalies (2019)** applies a Hotelling T² control chart to citrus-leaf image features.
- **Mangrove species (2019)** uses transfer learning to tell three mangrove species apart from leaf shape.
- **Pigment prediction (2018)** compares convolutional networks that predict chlorophyll, carotenoid, and anthocyanin from multispectral images.
- **Cacao leaf color (2019)** compares ANFIS with ANN and Fuzzy-MLP for separating fine-flavor from bulk cacao.

Results for each study are on the individual publication pages.
