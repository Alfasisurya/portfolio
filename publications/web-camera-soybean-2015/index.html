Web camera as low cost multispectral sensor for quantification of chlorophyll in soybean leaves

  • AuthorsMarcelinus A. S. Adhiwibawa, Yonathan E. Setiawan, Kestrilia R. Prilianti, Tatas H. P. Brotosudarmo
  • VenueInternational Seminar on Photonics, Optics, and Its Applications (ISPhOA 2014), Proc. SPIE 9444
  • CitationProc. SPIE 9444, 94440G
  • DOI10.1117/12.2080951
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

Plants were imaged from above inside an acquisition box. NDVI from the camera had a coefficient of determination of 0.70 against SPAD. Leaf-area error was 18.41 percent.
