Low-cost chlorophyll meter (LCCM): portable measuring device for leaf chlorophyll

  • AuthorsEvan Hutomo E. P., Marcelinus Alfasisurya S. Adhiwibawa, Kestrilia R. Prilianti, Heriyanto, Tatas H. P. Brotosudarmo
  • VenueSecond International Seminar on Photonics, Optics, and Its Applications (ISPhOA 2016), Proc. SPIE 10150
  • CitationProc. SPIE 10150, 101500V
  • DOI10.1117/12.2243607
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

On 20 cassava leaves, with a SPAD-502 as the reference, the paper reports a coefficient of determination of 0.9681 and an RMSE of 0.014. The output is an NDVI-style combination of the red and near-infrared channels, sent over Bluetooth to an app named NDVI Reader.
