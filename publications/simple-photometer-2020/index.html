Simple photometer development for educational purposes in natural pigment analysis

  • AuthorsJovine Marcella Kurniawan, Marcelinus A. S. Adhiwibawa
  • VenueIndonesian Journal of Natural Pigments
  • CitationIndonesian Journal of Natural Pigments, 2(1), 17–20
  • DOI10.33479/ijnp.2020.02.1.17
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

The instrument uses a 633 nm LED, a TSL250 photodiode, a 3D-printed cuvette holder, and an Arduino Uno for acquisition. Against a UV-Vis 1800 spectrophotometer the paper reports a coefficient of determination of 0.98, with LOD and LOQ absorbance of 0.0267 and 0.0344.
