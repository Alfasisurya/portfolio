Statistical analysis for evaluating natural yellow coloring agents from peel of local fruits in Malang: mangosteen, honey pineapple and red dragon fruits

  • AuthorsYehezkiel Steven Kurniawan, Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa, Edi Setiyono, Muhammad Riza Ghulam Fahmi, Hendrik O. Lintang
  • VenueIndonesian Journal of Natural Pigments
  • CitationIndonesian Journal of Natural Pigments, 1(2), 49–52
  • DOI10.33479/ijnp.2019.01.2.49
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

Peels of mangosteen, honey pineapple, and red dragon fruit were extracted in water, acetone, and ethanol. Ethanolic and acetone extracts of mangosteen peel were strong yellow and not statistically different. The ethanolic mangosteen extract had the lowest L* and the highest b*.
