An Indonesian Marine Bacterium, Pseudoalteromonas rubra, Produces Antimicrobial Prodiginine Pigments

  • AuthorsEdi Setiyono, Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa, Renny Indrawati, Monika Nur Utami Prihastyanti, Yuzo Shioi, Tatas Hardo Panintingjati Brotosudarmo

  • DOI10.1021/acsomega.9b04322
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar
MetadataVolume and pages follow the CV and the Google Scholar profile. The DOI is printed on the PDF.

Strains PS1 and SB14 were isolated from two sites on Alor Island. The paper reports cycloprodigiosin, prodigiosin, and 2-methyl-3-hexyl-prodiginine as major pigments, plus three minor prodiginine derivatives that differ in alkyl-chain length. In a disk-diffusion test at 20 μg/mL, cycloprodigiosin showed the greatest inhibition of *Staphylococcus aureus*, 25.1 ± 0.55 mm. The PDF in the working files is not hosted here.
