Automatic leaf color level determination for need based fertilizer using fuzzy logic on mobile application: a model for soybean leaves

  • AuthorsKestrilia R. Prilianti, Samuel P. Yuwono, Marcelinus A. S. Adhiwibawa, Monika N. P. Prihastyanti, Leenawaty Limantara, Tatas H. P. Brotosudarmo
  • Venue2014 6th International Conference on Information Technology and Electrical Engineering (ICITEE), Yogyakarta
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

The paper replaces a laboratory leaf-tissue test with a non-destructive color reading. Fuzzy logic converts the image into a leaf color level intended to guide fertilizer need.
