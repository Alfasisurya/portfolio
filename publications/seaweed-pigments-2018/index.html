Composition of the main dominant pigments from potential two edible seaweeds

  • AuthorsTatas Hardo Panintingjati Brotosudarmo, Heriyanto, Yuzo Shioi, Indriatmoko, Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa, Renny Indrawati, Leenawaty Limantara
  • VenuePhilippine Journal of Science
  • CitationPhilippine Journal of Science, 147(1), 47–55
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

Both seaweeds are used for carrageenan or alginate. The paper measures their chlorophyll and carotenoid composition by reverse-phase HPLC, which the food-use literature had largely left undescribed.
