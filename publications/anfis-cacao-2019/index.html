Adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference system for non-linear classification problem of Theobroma cacao image

  • AuthorsKestrilia Rega Prilianti, Hendry Setiawan, Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa, Indah Anita-Sari, Syaiful Anam, Tatas Hardo Panintingjati Brotosudarmo

Fine-flavor and bulk cacao are usually separated only after years of growth. The paper uses leaf color, which earlier work had linked to the two groups, and reports that the ANFIS classifier was more robust than ANN and Fuzzy-MLP on the experiment described.
