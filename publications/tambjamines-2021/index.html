Characterization of tambjamines pigment from marine bacterium Pseudoalteromonas sp. PM2 indigenous from Alor Island, Indonesia

  • AuthorsEdi Setiyono, Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa, Matheus Randy Prabowo, Tatas Hardo Panintingjati Brotosudarmo
  • VenueIndonesian Journal of Natural Pigments
  • CitationIndonesian Journal of Natural Pigments, 3(1), 16–23
  • DOI10.33479/ijnp.2021.03.1.16
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

Six yellow-pigmented strains were isolated. UV-Vis and FTIR spectra of the crude extracts matched tambjamines. Separation gave five tambjamine types with maximum absorbance from 374 to 392 nm. Strain PM2 was assigned as Pseudoalteromonas sp. PM2, accession LC505058.
