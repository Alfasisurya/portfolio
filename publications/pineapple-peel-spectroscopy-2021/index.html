Spectroscopy study of honey pineapple peels extracted in different solvents

  • AuthorsYehezkiel Steven Kurniawan, Edi Setiyono, Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa, Krisfian Tata Aneka Priyangga, Leny Yuliati
  • VenueIndonesian Journal of Natural Pigments
  • CitationIndonesian Journal of Natural Pigments, 3(1), 32–35
  • DOI10.33479/ijnp.2021.03.1.32
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

The water extract was consistent with polar flavonoid or steroid compounds. The ethanol extract was consistent with polar carotenoids and showed no fluorescence. The acetone extract contained carotenoids and a trace of chlorophyll, with an emission signal at 670 nm.
