Detection of anomalies in citrus leaves using digital image processing and T2 Hotelling multivariate control chart

  • AuthorsMarcelinus A. S. Adhiwibawa, Waego Hadi Nugroho, Solimun
  • Venue2019 International Conference of Artificial Intelligence and Information Technology (ICAIIT)
  • DOI10.1109/ICAIIT.2019.8834453
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

The paper starts from the practical problem that citrus disease is usually judged by looking at leaves in the field. Image features are monitored with a multivariate Hotelling T2 chart rather than by a single color threshold.
