Analysis of hotels spatial clustering in Bali: density-based spatial clustering of application with noise (DBSCAN) algorithm approach

  • AuthorsAchmad Fauzan, Afdelia Novianti, Raden Rara Mentari Ayu Ramadhani, Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa

The question is where hotels cluster, in the context of the tourism shock from COVID-19. Quadrant counts and Ripley's K test the point pattern. DBSCAN then forms the clusters.
