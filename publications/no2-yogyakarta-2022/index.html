Interpolasi polutan nitrogen dioksida (NO2) di Kota Yogyakarta dengan pendekatan ordinary kriging dan inverse distance weighted

  • AuthorsMuthia Citra Safira, Achmad Fauzan, Marcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa
  • VenueJurnal Aplikasi Statistika & Komputasi Statistik
  • CitationJurnal Aplikasi Statistika & Komputasi Statistik, 14(2), 55–66
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

English rendering of the title: interpolation of nitrogen dioxide in Yogyakarta by ordinary kriging and inverse distance weighting. The city measured NO2 at some sites only. On the comparison in the paper, ordinary kriging had an RMSE of 0.4847 and inverse distance weighting had an RMSE of 0.5224.
