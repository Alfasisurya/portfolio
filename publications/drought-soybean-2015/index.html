Application of simple multispectral image sensor and artificial intelligence for predicting of drought tolerant variety of soybean

  • AuthorsMarcelinus Alfasisurya Setya Adhiwibawa, Yonathan Eric Setiawan, Yusuf Setiawan, Kestrilia Rega Prilianti, Tatas Hardo Panintingjati Brotosudarmo
  • VenueProcedia Chemistry
  • CitationProcedia Chemistry, 14, 246–255
  • DOI10.1016/j.proche.2015.03.035
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

The sensor, built from a web camera, records leaf area, plant height, and a chlorophyll-related signal. Fuzzy logic and an artificial neural network were compared. The neural network predicted the drought-tolerant class with an accuracy of about 80 percent.
