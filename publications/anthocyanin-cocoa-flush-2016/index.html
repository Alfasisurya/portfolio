Anthocyanin and chlorophyll content in flush as selection markers on fine flavor cocoa (Theobroma cacao L.)

  • AuthorsIndah Anita-Sari, Sobir, Irvan Faizal, Agung Wahyu Susilo, Marcelinus A. S. Adhiwibawa, R. A. P. Nugroho
  • VenuePelita Perkebunan
  • CitationPelita Perkebunan, 32(3), 171–180
  • ScholarSearch this title on Google Scholar

Flush samples came from the Kaliwining germplasm collection. Pigment analysis was done at the Ma Chung Research Center for Photosynthetic Pigments. The design was a completely randomized comparison of four fine-flavor and five bulk genotypes.
